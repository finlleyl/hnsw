# G3: tombstone walk budget

Search stops after expanding `deadBudget` tombstones on layer 0 and counts the stop in
`Stats.BudgetStops`. This gate measures how many tombstones searches expand, then
checks recall and latency with the budget in place.

**Setup.** darwin/arm64, Apple M4 Pro, go1.26.2, one goroutine. Clustered synthetic data
(the generator in `index_test.go`), n=100k, 1000 held-out queries, M=16, EfConstruction
200, seed 7, k=10, tie-aware recall@10 against brute force over live keys. "30% groups"
deletes whole groups of the nearest of 64 random pivots until at least 30% of keys are
gone (32.6% at d=128, 30.9% at d=768). "99% dead" then deletes random keys until 99% are
gone. Latency is wall time per search from the internal search entry point, so it leaves
out validation and result allocation. Expanded tombstones were counted by a temporary
counter in a copy of the code, not in this repository.

No search reached the budget, and no search returned short results.

## Result

Pass. At 30% groups, recall with the budget equals recall without it. At 99% dead, d=768,
ef=100, p99 latency is 14.8 ms against the 250 ms limit. The constant stays at 65536,
about four times the largest count measured (17041), so at this scale it is only a
backstop. It bounds the walk on larger or more heavily deleted indexes, which were not
measured: n above 100k, real embeddings and amd64 are open.
